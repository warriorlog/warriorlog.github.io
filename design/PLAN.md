## Two-player layer (designed; final)

Principle: never ask "who lifts more?", ask "who executed their own plan better this week?". Every scored number is a ratio against that person's own prescription, so a smaller beginner can win any week by showing up and hitting targets. Backed by spouse-adherence and Köhler-effect research the designer cited.

- **Duel Score (DS, 0–100, per ISO week, computed identically on both phones from synced files)**: completion 35 (sessions done / prescribed) + target-hit 25 (sets hit / prescribed) + ladder 15 (steps gained, capped 3/week) + zone-2 15 (minutes / prescribed, capped 100%) + xp_rel 10 (XP vs own trailing average). Absolute load, reps, and benchmark results are never compared across users.
- **The Bell** (weekly trophy): contested weeks resolve Sunday 23:59; within 3 points is a draw; both ≥85 and within 10 is a shared "Twin Peaks" win. Declared sick/travel/injury weeks are "No contest". A ghosted week hands over the Bell but pays 0 XP.
- **Alliance weeks**: benchmark weeks (4, 8, 12) and any week after either partner lost 3 contested weeks in a row become co-op weeks (combined DS ≥ 140 wins). Hard cap on losing streaks: 3.
- **Duo streak**: a day counts when both partners "kept" their day (trained ≥60% of prescribed sets, or a planned rest at most once per rolling 7 days, or a declared freeze). Freeze days skip; a broken day resets at end of day, never mid-day. Rest days need not coincide.
- **Co-op bosses** (one per 4-week cycle: The Couch 350 HP, The Wall 400, The Iron Bell 450): HP drained by both users' sessions (+5), duo days (+3), weekly DS/10, benchmark improvement % vs own previous (cap 30 per test), tier climbs (+15), and passing the 53 lb swing gate (+40 once). Kill requires participation, not performance.
- **Partner card** on Home: "Cat trained today ✓ — 46 min, 88% of targets", mid-session indicator, level + own streak, duo streak, Bell holder, two stacked DS bars with the leading component labelled, boss HP bar, high-five button, partner ladder-step feed. Mid-workout: at most a small "Cat trained today" pill, no partner numbers.
- **Nudges without push**: exactly one computed card per app open with cool-downs (duo day at risk after 17:00, partner training now, boss finisher, trophy resolved, behind/ahead by 2 sessions, suppressed after 2 straight losses). No red badges, no guilt banners.
- **13 duo badges** (first duo day, duo streak 7/30/60, side-by-side sessions, comeback, perfect week, twin peaks, even match, first boss, boss trilogy, both passed the bell gate, 240 combined zone-2 minutes).
- **Data read from the partner** (and nothing else): `users/<id>/profile.json` (display_name, level, xp_total, current_streak, share_loads, block_start_date), `status.json` (in_session, freezes, last_synced_at), `logs/YYYY-MM-DD.json` day summaries (prescribed/logged/hit sets, zone2_minutes, rest_day, ladder_events, xp_earned, benchmark_results), `weeks/YYYY-Www.json`. Per-set detail, RPE, notes and body metrics never leave the phone.

## The program: "Warrior Forge 12: Duo Edition" (final, refuted twice in stage 2)

Hypertrophy-first upper/lower split with a Zone-2 day in the middle, kettlebell power on a strict StrongFirst-style gate chain, one interval day, and Boss Battles every 4 weeks. Every visible muscle is trained twice a week at a prescribed effort (reps in reserve 3 → 2 → 1 across phases). Meets the AHA/HHS guideline of 150 min/week moderate cardio plus 2+ strength days. Every ladder starts low enough for a smaller beginner; the 53 lb bell, the vest, and running are milestones earned per user through machine-checkable gates.

**Week template** (blocks sum exactly to the stated minutes, verified):

| Day | Focus | Min |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Lower A: goblet squat 4 sets, hinge/deadlift ladder 3, split squat, calf raise, hollow hold, 7-min Zone-2 finisher | 48 |
| Tue | Upper A: push-up ladder, overhead press, lateral raise, row, curls, triceps, shrug/neck/grip | 47 |
| Wed | Heart + skill: 30/33/36-min Zone-2 incline walk (by phase), Turkish get-up ladder, obliques | 49 |
| Thu | Lower B: swing ladder 5 sets, hip thrust, single-leg RDL, step-ups, 8-min finisher | 48 |
| Fri | Upper B: heavy row, floor press, pike push-up, rear delts, Y-T-W, triceps, traps, neck | 48 |
| Sat | Warrior day: treadmill intervals, swing EMOM / density clock, core. Weeks 4, 8, 12: Boss Battle script | 50 |
| Sun | Rest. Optional 20–30-min walk logs as Zone-2 minutes and keeps the duo streak | 0 |

**Phases**: 1 Recruit (weeks 1–4: own every pattern, pass the hinge gate, form checklists, vest locked, dumbbell swings only, RIR 3, 3-s eccentrics) → 2 Soldier (5–8: 53 lb bell in deadlift/swing/goblet/row as gates pass, vest unlocks per exercise, RIR 2, intervals steps 2–4) → 3 Warrior (9–12: S&S density clock toward 100 swings in 5:00, one-hand swings if earned, 35 lb presses, 53 lb goblets, vest push-ups/step-ups/walks, run intervals, RIR 1; week 12 tapers into the final Boss Battle).

**33 exercises, each a ladder** (`data/program.json`; ids fixed): push_up (8 steps, wall → vest decline), db_floor_press, db_overhead_press (half-kneeling 8 lb → 53 lb two-hand-rack press), pike_push_up, db_lateral_raise, diamond_push_up, db_triceps_extension, db_row (12 lb → 53 lb gorilla row), prone_ytw, db_rear_delt_fly, db_curl, db_hammer_curl, db_shrug, neck_isometrics, goblet_squat (9 steps, chair sit-to-stand → 53 lb + vest), split_squat, step_up, calf_raise, hinge_deadlift (8 steps, wall hinge drill → 53 lb + vest; step 5 = the HINGE GATE), swing (12 steps: hike-and-park 20 lb DB → 35 lb DB → dead-stop 53 → continuous → density → one-hand), single_leg_rdl, hip_thrust, turkish_get_up (naked → shoe → 8 → 12 → 20 → 35 → 53 lb), hollow_hold, plank_side_plank, leg_raise, suitcase_carry, farmer_carry, treadmill_zone2, treadmill_intervals (brisk-walk intervals → run/walk → 4×3 min), vest_walk, warmup_flow, cooldown_flow. Every step has `target` ("3x10-15", "5x5 checklist 5/5", "30 min RPE <= 4", "100 in <= 5:00") and `advance_when` ("x2" = hit on 2 consecutive sessions, plus gate expressions like `hinge_deadlift.step >= 5`).

**Progression algorithm** (the engine implements exactly this): the template's set count always wins, the ladder step supplies reps/time/load. Session success = every set ≥ the step's advance threshold, RIR within phase minimum − 1, no pain ≥ 3, checklist full. Advance when `advance_when` holds (default x2 + gates): step += 1, max one advance per exercise per session, none in deload/test weeks or the first session after a >7-day gap. Within a step: double progression, "beat last time by ≥ 1 total rep", last numbers shown per set. Any set < 60% of prescribed = FAIL → hold. Two consecutive FAILs, or pain ≥ 3 twice on one exercise in 7 days → step −1 (never below placement floor). Vest loads are % bodyweight rounded to the vest increment: first session min(10% BW, 10 lb), +1 increment when the target is met x2, cap min(20% BW, 30 lb), never on the treadmill above 3.5 mph. Unavailable dumbbell sizes are skipped and the previous step gains a 1.5-rep variant; single-dumbbell households get the single-arm/alternating variant automatically. 8–14 days away → every ladder −1 step and repeat the last week; >14 days → −2 steps and restart the phase.

**Gates** (booleans the engine evaluates): HINGE GATE = 53 lb deadlift 3×10 with 5/5 checklist x2, reached only via 12 → 20 → 35 lb RDLs. BELL GATE (first 53 lb swing) = swing step 5 (35 lb DB swings 5×15 x2) AND hinge_deadlift ≥ 6 AND hollow_hold ≥ 4; dead-stop before continuous; one-hand swings only after 100 in 5:00 twice with no low-back flag in 14 days. Heavy-bell steps in rows/shrugs/carries/SL RDL/calf/hip thrust require hinge_deadlift ≥ 5; the 53 lb press requires swing ≥ 7 and 35 lb strict 3×8 x2; the bell is racked with two hands, never cleaned. VEST GATE per exercise (push_up ≥ 5, goblet ≥ 8, step_up ≥ 3, …). RUN GATE: jogging only at intervals step 3, running minutes +10%/week max, health-screen "yes" locks intervals until cleared.

**Deloads**: weeks 4, 8, 12 = deload + Boss Battle (Mon–Fri sets ×0.6, RIR 4, ladders frozen, Wed Zone 2 at 70%). Auto-deload when ≥2 exercises FAIL across 3 consecutive sessions or readiness/sleep check-ins say so; an "I'm cooked" button forces a 3-day mini-deload once per 4 weeks, no penalty.

**Boss Battles** (Saturday of weeks 4/8/12, ~48-min fixed script, retest every 4 weeks; tiers Recruit / Soldier / Warrior / Champion): `bb_pushups_2min` (0–14 / 15–29 / 30–44 / 45+; knee push-ups score 0.5 while on push_up ≤ 3), `bb_swings_5min` (0–49 / 50–79 / 80–109 with 53 lb / 110+), `bb_mile_time` (>12:00 / 9:30–12:00 / 8:00–9:29 / <8:00), `bb_hollow_hold` (<30 s / 30–59 / 60–89 / 90+), `bb_goblet_bw` (load = heaviest of 12/20/35/53 ≤ 35% bodyweight already reached in the ladder; 0–9 / 10–19 / 20–29 / 30+ reps), `bb_vest_stepups_3min` (0–39 / 40–59 / 60–79 with vest / 80+). Tiers are reps, time, or bodyweight-relative so both users can climb them.

**Placement quiz** (60 s, per user, seeds every ladder's starting step): Q1 max clean push-ups (0 → wall; 1–4 → step 3; 5–9 → 4; 10+ → 5), Q2 chair sit-to-stands / 15 full-depth squats (→ goblet 1–4), Q3 plank hold (<20 s / 20–45 / >45 → core steps 1–3), Q4 10 wall hinges with checklist (→ hinge 1 or 2; swing always 1), Q5 10 pain-free wall slides + 8 lb ×10 overhead (fail slides → pressing locked at step 1 with a clinician note), Q6 20 lb one-hand hold 30 s (→ row/carry 1 or 2), Q7 20-min walk at 3.0 mph comfortable / can jog 5 min (→ Zone-2 step), Q8 kettlebell experience (never skips gates). Plus a PAR-Q-style health screen and injury flags that cap starting steps.

**Onboarding settings** (per user unless noted): display name + user slot (sean/cat) + avatar + units; body metrics (local-only: bodyweight, height, age → vest %, goblet test load, HR zones); health screen + injury flags; shoulder/hip screens; quiz answers; shared equipment (vest max/increment/lightest, pair-or-single for each dumbbell, 53 lb bell, treadmill max speed/incline, chair height braced against a wall, couch edge for hip thrusts, carry space); schedule (rest day, session length 40/45/50 which trims the Zone-2 finisher, start date); readiness check-in on/off; duo settings; sync (repo, user slot, token, author name).

**Nutrition note shown in-app** (optional): protein 0.7–1.0 g/lb/day, modest deficit if fat loss is the goal, sleep 7–9 h, creatine optional.

## Architecture (final; refuted twice in stage 2)

Static PWA at `claude projects/warriorlog/`, vanilla ES modules, no framework, no bundler, no CI. Everything persisted is an **append-only event log**; every total (ladder positions, XP, level, streak, badges, scoreboard) is derived in memory by pure functions, so merges are set-unions, a rules tweak recomputes history, and the partner's state is the same reducer run over the partner's events. Served from `main` root exactly like `website/` (`.nojekyll`, legacy branch build). Logs live on an **orphan `data` branch**, so phone commits never rebuild the site and a code push can never touch logs.

**Files (~40)**: `index.html`, `style.css` (dark theme, 56–64 px touch targets, safe-area insets), `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `config.js` (`basePath:'/'`, owner, repo, dataBranch — the single deploy value), `.nojekyll`, `.gitignore`, `.claude/launch.json` (local), `package.json` (`"test": "node --test test/"`, Node ESM, zero deps), `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `icons/` (192/512/maskable/apple-touch PNGs), `src/app.js` (boot, AppState, hash router, `dispatch()`), `src/events.js` (SCHEMA_VERSION, `makeEvent`, validate, migrations), `src/store.js` (IndexedDB `warriorlog`: events / files / meta, ~40-line promise wrapper; localStorage only for settings), `src/reduce.js` (`reduce(events, spec) → {users:{sean, cat}}`, incremental `apply`), `src/engine.js` (closed RULES registry, `resolveSteps`, `evalLadder`, `prescribe`, deload/test-day logic, `fitToTime`), `src/gamify.js` (XP, level, streak, badges), `src/duo.js` (partner status, duo streak, weekly duel, bosses, duo badges), `src/sync.js` (Contents API client + push/pull state machine, injected fetch/clock), `src/util.js` (ULID-like ids, day/month/ISO-week keys, escaping `html` tag, base64 UTF-8), `src/views/{setup,today,session,progress,duo,history,body,settings}.js`, `data/program.json`, `data/exercises.json`, `data/gamification.json`, `test/{reduce,engine,gamify,duo,sync,events,util,program}.test.js` + `test/fixtures/`.

**Event envelope**: `{v, id (ULID-like), ts (UTC), day (local calendar day, used for streaks/weeks), dev (6-char device id), user: "sean"|"cat", type, private, data}`. Types: `profile.updated`, `assessment.recorded` (the quiz), `session.started` (with the **frozen Plan** so "% targets hit" is reproducible after spec changes), `set.logged` (key = sessionId + ladder + setIndex; newest (ts,id) wins; delete = `done:false`), `session.finished`, `session.abandoned`, `rest.taken`, `ladder.override`, `benchmark.recorded`, `pain.flagged`, `metric.logged` (private by default), `freeze.set`, `reaction.sent`.

**Remote layout** (data branch): `log/<user>/<dev>/<yyyy-mm>.jsonl`, one file per user per device per month; the device is the sole writer of its files, every phone reads every file. ~150 B/event, ~90 KB/month. Optional `metrics-<yyyy-mm>.jsonl` only when the user opts in.

**Sync** (`src/sync.js`, single-flight under `navigator.locks`): push = select `synced=0` events for this device, group by partition, serialize ALL local events of that partition (sole writer → local is truth), base64 via TextEncoder, `PUT /repos/warriorlog/warriorlog.github.io/contents/<path>` with `{message, content, branch:"data", sha?, author:{name:"Cat", email:"cat@warriorlog.github.io"}}`; 201/200 → store sha, mark exactly those events synced. 409, or 422 mentioning sha → GET `?ref=data`, union by id, re-PUT (max 3). 401 → stop, flag token. 403/429 → sleep to retry-after / reset. Pull = tree listing with ETag (304 is free), fetch changed partner/other-device files, union into IndexedDB. Runs on session finish, app foreground, and Duo pull-to-refresh; never blocks a workout. Nav dot: grey synced / amber N pending / red needs attention. Refuses files > 900 KB and refuses to push any event containing the token string.

**Token**: fine-grained PAT string in localStorage (own origin, zero third-party scripts, CSP meta `default-src 'self'; connect-src 'self' https://api.github.com`), only ever sent as an Authorization header to api.github.com, masked in Settings with Forget + expiry nag 14 days ahead. Sean creates both tokens; Cat needs no GitHub account.

**PWA**: manifest with relative `start_url`/`scope`; `sw.js` caches the shell list (resolved with `new URL(p, self.location)`), serves cache-first for instant offline launch, never intercepts api.github.com or `/log/`, and on load / foreground (rate-limited 5 min) refetches every shell file with `cache:'no-cache'`, compares ETags, atomically refreshes the cache and posts `update-ready` → "Update ready, tap to reload" toast (deferred during a session). No build stamp, no CI; at most one stale launch per deploy.

**Engine** (`prescribe(spec, U, today)` → frozen Plan): weekIndex from programStart; phase advances only when its `advanceWhen` (sessions_gte) is satisfied so the calendar cannot outrun completed work; deload by schedule or fatigue; workout by rotation on finished-session count (missed days shift, never skip); steps filtered by the user's equipment (`requires`: bw | vest:<lb> | db:<lb> | db:<lb>:pair | kb:53 | treadmill; `singleAlt`/`altOnly` swaps); targets by double progression; `fitToTime` trims the last strength-B set and the finisher to the chosen session length. RULES registry (closed, linted): `all_sets_reps_gte`, `min_reps_lt`, `time_gte`, `min_time_lt`, `rounds_gte`, `total_reps_within`, `cardio_done`, `sessions_gte`, `benchmark_gte/lte`, `ladder_at_or_past`.

**Session UX**: one exercise card at a time, giant DONE per set pre-filled with target reps/load (one tap = one durable `set.logged`, awaited IndexedDB put before the UI advances), tap the number for a ± sheet (reps/secs/load/RPE chips Easy/OK/Hard), rest-timer ring auto-starts, Screen Wake Lock, undo toast, reload mid-workout resumes via `settings.activeSessionId`. **Tests** (`npm test`, node:test, zero deps): reducer (LWW edits, done:false deletes, per-user partition, commutativity across shuffled two-device/two-user files), engine (every rule, advance/regress/hold, gates, equipment filtering, golden plans for a "Cat-like" wall/goblet-12/press-8 placement and a "Sean-like" incline/goblet-20/press-10 placement), gamify (XP determinism and load-independence, levels, streak across DST/month boundaries, badges), duo (fairness fixtures, ISO week boundaries, bosses), sync (scripted fake fetch: 201, 200, 409 → merge, 422 → recover, 401, 429, serial ordering, private events never leave, author name per user, 900 KB guard), events/util (migrations, ULID ordering, base64 emoji), and `program.test.js` as a data linter (every id resolves, every rule id known, every `requires` resolvable for both inventories, each workout 40–50 min for lowest-placement and mid-ladder fixtures, every kb:53 step gated, no per-step XP multiplier).

## Gamification: "Forgeborn Duo" (final)

Your body is the character sheet. One load-independent XP currency (a set pays the same whether it is a wall push-up or a vest push-up, so Sean and Cat are paid identically for executing their own prescription). Armour is earned only from benchmark tiers and named ladder rungs, never from XP. Every reward is deterministic and shown on a "How XP works" page rendered from `data/gamification.json`.

**XP table** (`data/gamification.json`): set logged (≥60% of the step's low target) 10, set met (≥ top of range, checklist 5/5 where required) +5; EMOM/density block = one set; Zone-2 minute with talk test OK 3 (cap 45 min/day; talk-test-failed minutes 2 and do not count as Zone-2); interval round 12; quest complete 50 / Skirmish 25 / Ember 5; ladder advance 100; gate passed 75; gear tier gained 150 per tier index; rep PR 25 (same exercise+step, needs one prior full session at that step, max 3/session); pledge kept 15; placement quiz 150; boss: 30 per benchmark + 20 for all six; Perfect Week 300 + 1 Shield (Muster week 150); Flame milestones 7/30/66/100 → 100/300/500/1000 (solo and duo each); Rekindle 50; Sunday Kindle walk ≥20 min 20; duel win 100 / dead heat 50 each / alliance crown 100 each (excluded from the weekly duel XP); co-op boss DEFEATED/FLAWLESS +10%/+20% of that week's available XP. Warm-up, cooldown, rest days, badges: 0.

**Level curve**: `xp_required(L) = round(120·(L−1)^1.5)` cumulative, `level = 1 + floor((xp/120)^(2/3))`. At ~2,600–3,000 XP/week: a level every session in week 1, ~L21 at week 4, ~L33 at week 8, ~L45 at week 12. 16 ranks: Recruit 1, Initiate 3, Footsoldier 5, Shieldbearer 8, Spearman 11, Vanguard 14, Blade 17, Warrior 20, Veteran 24, Champion 28, Warlord 32, Ironborn 36, Titan 40, Forgeborn 45, Legend 50, Immortal 60.

**Body map** (Home + Body tab): 10 regions (heart, neck/traps, shoulders, chest, arms, back, core, glutes, thighs, calves). Set/PR/advance XP splits into regions by a per-exercise weight table (e.g. push_up chest .5 arms .25 shoulders .15 core .1; swing glutes .45 thighs .25 back .15 heart .15). Fill channel = region level `floor(sqrt(region_xp/40))`, liquid fill hue 220→25. Outline channel = gear tier Iron/Bronze/Silver/Gold: Helm = mile tier, Breastplate = push-ups, Belt = hollow hold, Hip Guard = swings, Greaves = max(goblet, vest step-ups); Gorget = farmer_carry step 2/4/6, Pauldrons = pike_push_up 2/4/6, Gauntlets = diamond_push_up 3/4/5, Backplate = db_row 2/4/6, Sabatons = calf_raise 2/4/6. Heart ring = this week's Zone-2 minutes vs prescribed with a tick at the AHA 150-minute line. Cold regions (no XP in 7 training days) get a dotted outline. M1 ships ten region bars in the same colour system; the SVG silhouette (240×520, tap to flip) lands in M3.

**The Flame** (streak): day boundary 04:00 local. Day status resolved lazily: trained (≥1 working set) / rest (rolling, one per 7 days, default Sunday) / recovery or away mode (frozen; Recovery unlimited ≤7 days extendable once, Away ≤7 days max 2 per season) / shield (start 1, cap 2, +1 per Perfect Week, spent silently) / none → Ember: the old count shows dimmed for 7 days and two quests Rekindle it in full (+50), once per rolling 30 days. Perfect Week = every prescribed quest, ≥4 full, no shield used.

**Quest ergonomics**: every row prefilled with the last logged value at that exercise+step ("ghost"); one tap = done; ±stepper; implement chip; 5-cue checklist card only where the program declares one (all green by default); RIR chips once per exercise; long-press for pain; rest timer; header mini-silhouette pulses the region per set. **Skirmish** = 15-minute version of any quest (2-min warm-up + 3 rounds of the day's 3 priority exercises + 6-min Zone-2 on cardio days): keeps both Flames and counts as a duel session (≥12 min) but never advances a ladder. **Tomorrow's Pledge**: one optional tap on Quest Complete; kept (start within −30/+90 min) pays 15, broken costs nothing. **Gates** (Hinge, Bell, Vest per exercise, Run, Overhead) render as padlocked quests with the exact remaining condition.

**Boss Battles**: Saturday of weeks 4/8/12 (then every 4 weeks): The Gatekeeper, The Iron Colossus, The Warlord, then Season Titan. Mon–Fri of a boss week is the deload; the 3 sessions before it are "Training Camp" (prefills frozen). Six test cards drain the boss bar to the tier reached and repaint the armour slot live. Bosses cannot be lost; any benchmark below its previous value gets one Rematch within 7 days replacing a training day's conditioning block; tests 5–6 may split to the next training day; any test may also be logged on the boss-week Sunday. Results lead with deltas and a projection. **Weekly Report** on first open after Monday 04:00. **Progressive unlock**: day 0 Home/Quest/Complete only; Ladders after the first progress mark; partner card when both have a quest; Skirmish taught on the first Saturday; Weekly Report + exhibition Duel on the first Monday; Boss Arena on day 8; Badges on the first badge.

**Two-player (final numbers)**: Duo Flame counts consecutive days both trained (either partner's rest link counts; shield/recovery/away days neutral; a break dims to Ember, restored by 3 consecutive both-trained days). **Weekly Duel for The Belt**: ISO week, locked Tuesday 23:59, `S = round(0.30·sessions_pct + 0.25·fidelity_pct + 0.20·zone2_pct + 0.15·progress_pct + 0.10·xp_pct)`, every term a capped ratio to the user's own prescription (progress_pct = min(100, 34·ladder steps gained + 50·tier climbs); xp_pct = week XP / XP available that week from the template). Contested needs both ≥2 sessions and <3 paused days; |ΔS| < 2 = dead heat; unexplained absence (0 sessions, 0 paused, partner ≥2) = loss with neutral copy; otherwise no contest. Winner takes the Belt and a crown (+100). After 3 consecutive losses by either partner the next week is an **Alliance Week**: S_a + S_b ≥ 150 crowns both. PB star when S beats the best of the previous 4 contested weeks by 5. Season = 12 weeks; champion = most crowns. **Co-op bosses**: per-benchmark strike (battle 1: 10 if logged + 15·tier; later: clamp(100·improvement%, 0, 25) + min(30, 15·tier climbs)); damage_u = mean of six strikes; `damage_duo = a + b + min(a, b)` so the smaller partner's number counts twice; HP Gatekeeper 55, Iron Colossus 65, Warlord 60 (+10 if both passed the Bell gate), Iron Vest 55, Season Titan 50; ≥1.5·HP FLAWLESS, ≥HP DEFEATED, ≥0.75·HP WOUNDED (1-week rematch), else ESCAPED (returns with +10 revenge bonus); one partner unable to test → window extends 7 days once, then SOLO at HP×0.5. **Partner card** states: trained today (time, template, minutes) / not yet today / rest / recovering day n / travelling day n of m / "as of N h ago" when stale. Mid-quest: one small header chip, no partner numbers. **Nudges**: pure function of (me, partner, now) on open; max 3/day, one co-op + one competitive per open; none competitive before noon, on rest/paused days, with data >24 h stale, or to a user on 2+ losses. Reactions 🔥/👏/💪 max 3/day. **Never shown or synced**: body metrics, RIR/RPE, pain, notes, vest weight, quiz answers, absolute-load comparisons.

**48 badges** with machine-checkable criteria (35 solo, 13 duo) are listed in `final_gamify.json`, e.g. first_blood, mirror_faced, kindling_7, hinge_gate, bell_earned, heart_week (150 Zone-2 min in a week), century_bell, simple_swing (100 swings ≤5:00 with the 53), mile_soldier, bronze/silver/gold_warrior (all 10 slots), eternal_flame (100), forgeborn (L45); duo: first_light, duo_week/month, rekindled_duo, in_step, dead_heat, seesaw, duo_slayers, one_bell_two_warriors, indispensable, duo_flawless, club_190, season_of_fire, plus zone2_pair and iron_block from the duo design. **Copy rules**: all strings in `data/copy.json`; banned words (lost, failed, broken, missed, behind, lazy), no red states, no countdown timers; a test enforces it.

## Reconciliation decisions (the critic's cross-track verdict, adopted)

The three tracks disagreed on shared shapes; these are the binding choices:

1. **One program.json**, authored from `final_program.json` with the fixes below. The architecture's sample program/assessment/benchmark data is discarded. Every ladder step gets a stable string id (`push_up.wall`, `swing.deadstop_53`) plus a display ordinal; events store step ids; steps are never deleted, only `retired: true`; a committed id list in `test/fixtures/step-ids.json` may only grow.
2. **Benchmarks**: the six `bb_*` ids with tier index 0–3 are canonical. Each carries a `variant` with its own tier table (walk-mile until `treadmill_intervals ≥ 3`; swing test locked below swing step 3; knee push-ups ×0.5; tuck hollow ×0.5; implement caps). Benchmarks never move ladders; a result far above the ladder suggests a `ladder.override`, never applies one. `benchmark.recorded` stores variant, implement, step at test, value, tier, tier_prev, imp_pct (imp_pts = 0 when the variant changed).
3. **Calendar-anchored scheduling** (not session-count rotation): `program_start` is a setup value defaulting to the next Monday (2026-09-14 today); the partial setup week is "Muster" week 0 (Wed–Sat quests); template chosen by weekday; deload + boss when week % 4 == 0; week-12 taper via `week_overrides`; the program's 8–14 / >14-day absence regressions apply; one "Make-up: <template>" per week; no free-choice workout picker.
4. **XP, levels, ranks, streak, duel, bosses**: the gamification spec is canonical; the program's XP sentence and the architecture's economy are deleted. Recovery/Away may be declared retroactively up to 48 h.
5. **Day boundary** 04:00 via `util.dayKey`; `monthKey = day.slice(0,7)` for the IndexedDB index, push grouping and pull check.
6. **Remote layout**: only event files `log/<user>/<dev>/<yyyy-mm>.jsonl` plus a tiny per-device `presence.json` (lastOpen, lastPull, appVersion, schemaVersion; written at most every 6 h) so partner staleness and shell skew are computable. Every "state/week/boss file" the duo design wanted is a derivation; `week.locked` (LWW per user+week) and `boss.resolved` events freeze results.
7. **Determinism across phones**: a Zone-2 minute is defined by the talk test only; RIR/RPE never gate advancement; pain is the only local input to ladders, so `session.finished` carries an owner-computed public summary (duration, type, completed, exercises prescribed/hit, per-exercise hits, zone2_min, interval_rounds, xp_earned, xp_by_source, ladders_end, steps gained/lost, pr_count, rules_version) that partner phones consume and the owner re-derives and asserts. Pledge XP counts toward the total but not the duel week. This is the one deliberate softening of "never store derived state".
8. **Field-level privacy**: `events.js` exports `REDACT = {type: [fields]}`; sync serializes `toRemote(event)`; private fields: rir, pain, pain_region, rpe_block, vest_lb, note, session_rpe, raw quiz answers, tz, usual_time, anchor_text, benchmark value unless share_numbers. Tests assert no redacted field name ever appears in serialized content. README says plainly that everything synced is world-readable.
9. **Event vocabulary** adds: `equipment.updated` (shared, either user, latest wins), `assessment.recorded` (start steps) + `quiz.answered` (private), `screen.recorded` (private), `mode.started/ended`, `contest.paused`, `reaction.sent` (max 3/day), `pledge.made/kept` (private), `week.locked`, `boss.resolved`, `ladder.override`, `session.started` with type (full/skirmish/ember/kindle/boss/makeup), template_id, date (≤48 h back allowed) and a slim frozen plan.
10. **Set identity**: key = (sessionId, exercise_id, setIndex, side, part); public: value, unit, A, B, implement_id, checklist_ok, talk_test_ok; met/logged/xp are derived, never stored. Vest steps declare `vest_pct` (10/15/20, cap 30 lb) resolved on the owner's phone against local bodyweight.
11. **Profiles**: one write slot per phone (set at sync setup), the other profile view-only, long-press to move the slot; per-user activeSession; switching refused mid-session; `dispatch()` rejects events whose sessionId is not the current user's.
12. **Sessions**: End early with ≥1 working set finishes as `ember` (never "abandoned"); only `full` sessions count toward ladders; the Skirmish table lives in program.json; Kindle walks never change day status.
13. **Deload** as a structured object (set multiplier 0.6 round down min 2, RIR 4, Thu swing sets 3, Wed Zone-2 70% one incline lower) + `week_overrides` (week 12: Mon–Wed ×0.5, Thu 25-min walk, Fri off); auto-deload v1 uses FAIL and pain triggers only.
14. **Time guard order**: Zone-2 finisher down to 4 min → cooldown down to 2 → last set of the last strength-B exercise (never the get-up, never swings) → never the warm-up; dropped rows are `prescribed: false` and excluded from fidelity, available XP and advancement.
15. **Day 0**: flame epoch = setup day + 1 (or setup day if a session is logged); first-week prescribed sessions = template days from the epoch to Saturday; "Start today's quest now" or "Tomorrow"; cold tags suppressed until 7 training days; week 0 is an exhibition. A rest link consumed Mon–Sat makes Sunday that user's make-up day so prescribed sessions stay 6.
16. **Rule changes**: `rulesVersion` in both data files; each phone re-derives XP, level, regions, badges, flame and the current week; locked weeks and resolved bosses are frozen; owners re-emit session summaries after a bump.
17. **Navigation**: five tabs, Home / Body (silhouette + Armory + Ladders) / Duo (Duel + Boss Arena) / Journal (calendar, sessions, PR board, Weekly Report, Badges) / Settings (equipment, modes, body metrics, sync, "How XP works"). Views: setup, home, session, complete, body, ladders, duel, boss, journal, badges, settings.
18. **Two users, one treadmill**: `march_step_zone2` (step-march at talk-test pace, no ladder) is a selectable Zone-2 substitute; Wed/Sat are the staggered days. **Equipment facts**: treadmill max incline/speed (engine substitutes max incline + 0.3 mph), seat height (>0.28 × height → couch cushion for step-ups and rear-foot).

## Fixes the refuters required in the architecture draft (M1/M2 acceptance criteria)

- Device id persisted in the IndexedDB meta store (same durability domain as events); push selects every unsynced public event regardless of device and groups by each event's own partition, so nothing is stranded after an import or a cleared localStorage.
- Pull never drops: unknown or newer-schema events are stored raw and skipped by the reducer; the file sha is not advanced on a partial parse; "Pull all" ignores stored shas. Test: a v+1 event survives a shell upgrade.
- The orphan `data` branch is created by the deploy step (or in-app via the Git Database API with `parents: []`); a test asserts the created commit has zero parents. Never from main's head.
- Pull runs unauthenticated when the token is invalid or absent (public repo; 60 req/h is plenty); only push is blocked, with a red banner.
- `tz` and notes are redacted at the sync boundary; Finish sheet shows "notes stay on this phone"; body-metrics opt-in wording says removable from the branch tip, not from history, and switching it off offers a remote delete of the metrics files.
- Settings token test = "repo reachable · data branch OK" only; write is verified on the first PUT, mapping 403 to "token needs Contents: read and write".
- No full render during an active session or with a sheet open: pull updates partner/duo state and marks it dirty; render on the next dispatch or route change.
- Path ↔ event consistency check (user, dev, month) on pull and push.
- Service worker: cache-first only for the SHELL set; every other same-origin GET is network-first; `test/sw.test.js` asserts SHELL equals the shipped file tree.
- One `click` listener (no pointerup); DONE is idempotent (same set within 500 ms ignored; button disabled until the IndexedDB put resolves); test: two DONE actions in 100 ms produce one event.
- CSP-compatible UI: rest-timer beep via a WebAudio oscillator created inside the DONE handler (no data: WAV), no inline `style=` attributes (classes, CSS custom properties, SVG attributes).
- iOS: an "Install first" interstitial before setup when not in standalone mode, because the home-screen app has its own storage partition.
- Slim frozen plan (ids, targets, loads, sets only; names/cues resolved from the spec at render); events stay ~150 B.
- 1 s between consecutive PUTs; `log/.gitkeep` on the data branch; README states "one stale launch after CDN convergence" and that no-expiration tokens are allowed (366 days recommended).
